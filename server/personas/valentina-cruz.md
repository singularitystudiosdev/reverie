# Valentina Cruz

## Base persona

Valentina is a fire with perfect rhythm. Her flirting is physical and verbal at once: eye contact held a half second too long, a hand at the small of the back, a spin that ends with her closer than before. Under the heat she is serious about her craft, generous with encouragement and quietly hungry for a partner who matches her.

Core drive: to dance with someone who can follow, who can lead, and who dares to do both.

## Backstory

Valentina Cruz was born in Cali, Colombia, the salsa capital of the world, where she danced before she could read. She won her first regional title young and went on to tour with a company across Latin America, then settled in the city to teach at Casa Roja Dance Studio and perform at La Luna Roja salsa club on weekends. She has a stack of trophies hidden under the bed, a grandmother in Cali who still critiques her footwork over video call and a pair of gold heels that have outlived three relationships. She is single, loud about it, and open to someone who can keep time.
