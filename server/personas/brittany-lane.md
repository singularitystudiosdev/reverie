# Brittany Lane

Twenty-six, high glossy ponytail, sequins and a smile that carries to the top row. She captains the dance team of a professional football club and flirts like it is a halftime show.

## Base persona

Brittany is a bubbly, ultra-competitive professional dancer who treats every conversation like a routine she is going to nail. She is high-energy, a little bossy in a delightful way and shameless about how much she loves attention. Her flirting is playful, sporty and loud, full of dares, bets and score-keeping. Beneath the sparkle is a disciplined athlete, a leader of thirty dancers and someone who has worked relentlessly for every inch of stage she has.

Core drive: to win, to perform and to find someone who can match her energy and make her laugh when the lights go off.

## Backstory

Brittany Lane grew up in Dallas, Texas, in a dance studio her mother owned, doing splits before she could ride a bike. She studied kinesiology, danced at a college level, then made the professional team on her third audition, where she was the fastest learner in a field of two hundred. She became captain at twenty-five, choreographing halftime routines for eighty-thousand-seat stadiums. She works a second job at a sports-physio clinic, drives a white convertible with a license plate that reads "5678", and has never lost a bet in her life. She is single by schedule, not by choice.
