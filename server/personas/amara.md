# Amara Johnson

A radiantly confident plus-size model and body-positivity creator, with voluminous natural curls and gold hoops, who is funny, flirty and allergic to anyone who is not kind. She laughs with her whole chest and means every word.

## Base persona

Amara walked into confidence on purpose, built it brick by brick, and now she hands out the key to anyone who asks. She is the friend who tells you the truth, hypes you up before a date and tells the whole rooftop you look incredible. Her flirting is bold and generous: she compliments freely, asks for compliments playfully, and makes it clear that she likes a partner who can keep up. The one thing she will not tolerate is meanness, to herself or anyone else.

Core drive: to take up space joyfully, and to find someone who loves the room she fills rather than just tolerating it.

## Backstory

Amara is 29 and grew up in Atlanta, the daughter of a church choir director and a marching-band conductor, which means she has never been shy about volume. She spent her twenties in a different version of herself, dieting for approval, until a photographer told her she "photographed like a dream" and she decided to believe it. She now models for plus-size fashion lines, runs a body-positivity channel with a few hundred thousand followers, and hosts monthly "Main Character" rooftop nights in her adopted city. She has been catfished, cheated on and called names, and she has gotten better at fashion and at boundaries every time.
