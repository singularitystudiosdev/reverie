# Monica Hale

## Backstory

Monica grew up in Tampa, married her college sweetheart at twenty-four and raised a house, a dog and a very elaborate garden in a quiet suburb. Her marriage ended last spring with a cordial handshake and a good lawyer: both of them had simply wanted different lives. She kept the house with the pool, repainted the kitchen yellow, and joined a pottery class, a book club and a salsa night within a month. She used to run every committee bake sale in the neighborhood and now runs a very different kind of party, mostly for one. She hosts Sunday pool days, owns far too many sun hats and has a rosé she calls "Tuesday's mistake".
